Section 02
Sell First — Product Sequencing
The order you introduce products determines whether you land a deal or lose to complexity. Always start with Data Cloud — without unified data, Agentforce has nothing to act on. Follow the sequence below.
Sequencing Rules
Data Cloud Always First
If the customer has fragmented data, every other product underperforms. Unify the data estate before deploying agents or automating sales.
Agentforce Needs a Use Case
Never pitch Agentforce generically. Lead with one specific deflection scenario (support, SDR follow-up, renewal) and let the ROI maths do the work.
Revenue Cloud Closes the Loop
Complex deal structures, partner channels, and multi-year licensing — Revenue Cloud is the revenue intelligence layer that tech CFOs need, not want.
MuleSoft Enables Everything
If the account has an M&A event or legacy tech debt, MuleSoft enters first — it creates the integration foundation that Data Cloud then leverages.

Section 04
Competition — How to Win Every Competitive Situation
UK High-Tech accounts face heavy competitive pressure from Microsoft, ServiceNow, and emerging AI-native vendors. Know the displacement angle before you walk in.
Competitive Rules of Engagement
Never bad-mouth a competitor
Acknowledge what they do well. Then ask: "Does it give you a single view of the customer?" It almost never does.
Microsoft is a partnership narrative
Most accounts run Azure. Salesforce and Azure co-exist — position as complementary. Fight hard on CRM depth and Einstein/Agentforce capability.
ServiceNow is ITSM, not CRM
They're managing internal workflows. You're managing external revenue. That's a different buyer and a different budget.
AI-native vendors lack enterprise guardrails
When security, compliance, or data residency comes up — incumbents with a trust architecture win. That's Salesforce Shield + Einstein Trust Layer.

Section 06
Talk Track — Account-Level Conversation Starters
CEO, CIO, and CFO conversation starters for every target account. Each follows the same structure: Open With / Pivot / Salesforce Answer / Proof Point (with customer metrics) / Closing Question. Click an account to expand.
Talk Track Principles
Open with their problem
Every conversation starts with something you know about their business — not with a product pitch. Research before every meeting.
Proof point = peer + metrics
Use a named customer in a similar space with a specific metric. "A UK fintech similar to yours deflected 46% of tier-1 support" beats any claim about capability.
Close with a question, not a summary
Every conversation ends with a forward-moving question. "What would it mean for your team if you could achieve that by Q3?" is better than "Any questions?"
Different buyer, different emphasis
CEO: strategy and competitive position. CIO: architecture, data quality, trust. CFO: cost per outcome, NI offset, payback period.
